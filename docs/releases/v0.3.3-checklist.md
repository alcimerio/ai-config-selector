# v0.3.3 Release Checklist

`INCOMPLETE` never means success. Pre-tag authorization, tag-triggered
evidence, and public-release evidence are separate phases. Record only public,
sanitized facts; never record credentials, personal paths, account data,
signed URLs, generated policies, environment values, or raw terminal output.

## Relationship to the unpublished v0.3.0, v0.3.1, and v0.3.2 candidates

The `v0.3.0`, `v0.3.1`, and `v0.3.2` tags exist remotely and are never moved,
deleted, or reused. Required native macOS gates failed, so the
immutable-publication policy withheld all three Releases. v0.3.3 corrects the
identity-revalidation race exposed by the v0.3.2 tag run. The
[v0.3.0 checklist](v0.3.0-checklist.md),
[v0.3.1 checklist](v0.3.1-checklist.md), and
[v0.3.2 checklist](v0.3.2-checklist.md) remain historical records and do not
authorize or alter any tag, Release, or asset.
